\section{What}

\begin{frame}{What the thesis asks}
    \begin{block}{}
        How are investment logics \textbf{constructed}, \textbf{legitimised} and \textbf{contested} within the EIC instruments Pathfinder and STEP, and how can these dynamics be understood through the lens of institutional theory?
    \end{block}
    \vspace{4mm}
    \textbf{Object.} The text — EIC WP 2026, Pathfinder + STEP Scale-Up. Not evaluation of the instrument's performance.

    \vspace{3mm}
    \textbf{Anchors.} Draghi + Letta (2024); STEP integrated into the EIC; WP 2026 the first document to carry the integration \emph{at instrument level}.
\end{frame}

\section{Context management failure}
% =====================================================================

\begin{frame}{Context management failure}
    \textbf{Initial reading.} Corpus explicitly benchmarks against DARPA, Temasek, Chinese GGF.

    \vspace{2mm}
    \textbf{Corpus review.} No named comparator. Strategic Goal 4 says \enquote{USA and Asia}. Drift came from parallel literature (Mazzucato, Karo \& Kattel, Bonvillian) leaking into the primary-source reading.

    \vspace{3mm}
    \textbf{The reading was not wrong — the framing was.} DiMaggio \& Powell define mimetic isomorphism as \emph{structural resemblance}, not textual acknowledgement. Finding survived; framing corrected.

    \vspace{3mm}
    \textbf{What I would do differently.} Isolate primary-corpus reading sessions from secondary-literature sessions earlier. Provenance safeguard caught the drift late; earlier context discipline would have caught it at coding time.

    \vspace{2mm}
    \footnotesize Audit trail: synthesis §0.5.
\end{frame}

\section{Where next}
% =====================================================================

\begin{frame}{Where next --- back to the prior view}
    The study began from a prior view: abundant public, non-dilutive funding in Europe shifts the stage at which private capital enters --- private rounds arrive later, at similar absolute sizes, leaving firms under-capitalised at the stage they are then financed. The Work Programme text is consistent with the mechanism; the firm-level claim needs firm-level data.

    \vspace{3mm}
    \textbf{Empirical follow-up.} Firm-level financing-trajectory data on EIC-funded companies against non-EIC and non-EU comparators: round timing, round size, non-dilutive stack composition, capitalisation at the stage private capital enters.

    \vspace{2mm}
    \textbf{Theoretical follow-up.} Institutional-logics engagement with public-investment vehicles that reshape private-capital timing. The stage-shift as a logic-transmission mechanism, not only a funding statistic.

    \vspace{2mm}
    \textbf{Policy-design follow-up.} Whether STEP's demand-side turn (Seal, procurement chain) corrects the stage-shift or extends it --- instrument architecture and firm trajectory as one question.

    \vspace{3mm}
    \centering \footnotesize Thank you.
\end{frame}
