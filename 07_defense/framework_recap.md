## Scott (2014) — Three Pillars

| Pillar | Basis of compliance | Mechanism | Indicators in a text |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Regulative** | Expedience — sanctions | Coercion via rules | Regulations, budgets, deadlines, TRL requirements |
| **Normative** | Social obligation | Certification, appropriateness | "Should", DEI language, evaluation criteria |
| **Cognitive** | Taken-for-granted | Mimicry, common frames | Linear-innovation model, TRL as natural axis |

## DiMaggio & Powell (1983) — Isomorphism

| Type | Driver | Example in WP 2026 |
|---|---|---|
| **Coercive** | Power / dependence | Programme Manager authority; Commission conditionalities |
| **Mimetic** | Uncertainty | Structural resemblance to DARPA / Temasek / GGF (corpus benchmark is generic "USA and Asia" at Strategic Goal 4 — reading is structural per DP&P, not textual explicit-benchmark; see synthesis §0.5) |
| **Normative** | Professionalisation | Evaluator peer-expert norms; DEI compliance text |

## Thornton et al. (2012) — Four Logics

| Logic | Root metaphor | Sources of legitimacy | WP 2026 register |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Science** | Truth as method | Peer review, publication | Dominant at low TRL (Pathfinder) |
| **Market** | Transaction | Price signal, investor demand | Dominant at mid TRL; STEP claims |
| **State** | Redistribution | Political mandate, sovereignty | Dominant at high TRL; STEP access architecture |
| **Profession** | Expertise | Craft, accreditation | Present in evaluators; subordinated to State/Coercive |

Rule 7 (Codebook v2): `LOGIC_MARKET` — distinguish **register** (the document sounds market-y) from **organising** (the document actually organises market mechanisms).

## Suchman (1995) — Legitimacy

| Type | Basis |
|---|---|
| **Pragmatic** | Serves the audience's interest |
| **Moral** | Right thing to do |
| **Cognitive** | Taken-for-granted; unthinkable otherwise |

## The six patterns — one line each

- **A — Tension-Dissolution:** competing logics presented as complementary.
- **B — Coercive Governance Cluster:** Programme Manager soft-directive authority gradient.
- **C — State Social Equity Template:** standardised DEI compliance text anchored in state logic.
- **D — Challenge Chapter Logic Succession Template:** Science→Market→State reproduced intra-chapter.
- **E — STEP Circular Market Validation:** state constructs the market signal it then requires.
- **F — TRL as Cross-Pillar Legitimation Artefact:** `REG_TRL` + `COG_LINEAR` triple-load across regulative, cognitive, and mimetic axes.

Plus: **Sovereignty Seal** as field-bridging legitimacy artefact; **STEP hybrid instrument** (market mechanics on state access architecture); **Sustainability template not confirmed**.
