## Beat 4 — Findings (10 min, ~6–7 slides)

**Purpose:** show what the coding produced. Crescendo structure — start with the strongest finding, build to the most interesting artefact.

### 4.1 Headline finding (2 min, 2 slides)

- **Science → Market → State succession across TRL.** The whole WP organises this way. One slide with the visual (TRL axis + logic labels).
- **Pattern D — Challenge Chapter Logic Succession Template.** The succession is reproduced *inside each Challenge chapter* — not just aggregate, structural at multiple scales.

### 4.2 Pattern A + state-logic surprise (2:30 min, 1–2 slides)

- **Pattern A — Tension-Dissolution:** competing logics presented as complementary. Rule 1 (surface-visibility) keeps the code honest.
- **State-logic surprise (extension):** Pattern A operates at instrument level too. The EIC narrative uses market vocabulary (Venture Debt, equity, deal flow) but the underlying logic codes as state — rule 7 (vocabulary-vs-organising) is the codified check. Synthesis §2.6 anchors this at passage level; instrument level is the Discussion extension flagged here for continuity.
- Panel probe pre-empted: "isn't this just state aid dressed up as market?" — no. State aid is a legal category; Pattern A at instrument level is an institutional-legitimation move.

### 4.3 Pattern E — STEP circular market validation (2 min, 1 slide)

- State constructs the market signal it then requires: state capital → co-investment → treated as market signal → justifies more state capital.
- Rule 2 (STEP catalytic co-coding) codifies the read.
- Adjacent to critical blended-finance lit (Gabor, Mertens & Thiemann); arrived at through document-internal coding, not applied from outside.

### 4.4 Sovereignty Seal — the coded finding (2 min, 1 slide)

- Three coded functions: unlocks InvestEU Venture Debt ([152, 161]); unlocks BAS; awardable on NO GO ([166]).
- **Decoupling** from investment decision is the analytical door: state certifies strategic value independently of capital commitment.
- Field-bridging: portable legitimacy artefact travelling across "public EU programmes and private investors" (synthesis §2.8).
- Deliberately understated here — the Seal's *interpretation* is Discussion, not Findings. Findings gives you the artefact; Discussion asks what it's doing.

### 4.5 Brief mentions (1:30 min, 1 slide combining all)

- **Pattern B — Coercive Governance Cluster:** Programme Manager authority gradient; rule 3.
- **Pattern C — State Social Equity Template:** DEI standardised text; rule 4.
- **Pattern F — TRL as Cross-Pillar Legitimation Artefact:** triple-load across regulative + cognitive + mimetic.

## Beat 5 — Discussion — the centrepiece (5 min, ~3–4 slides)

**Purpose:** three moves — comparative frame, Seal-as-open-question, practice implications. This beat carries the presentation's originality.

### 5.1 STEP as incomplete developmental-state mimicry (2 min, 1 slide)

- Comparative slide: DARPA supply + demand (missions from DoD demand); Chinese GGF supply + demand (capital + SOE off-take); EU STEP supply only.
- **STEP borrows supply-side developmental-state mechanics but not the demand-side integration.** Structural feature of doing developmental-state work under Article 107 state-aid constraints.
- Comparison is structural (per DiMaggio-Powell's original meaning), not primary-empirical. Reading against Mazzucato / Li / Luong secondary lit + the corpus's own "USA and Asia" benchmark.
- **Answers LO #2 non-trivially** — this is the LO-#2 move I noted in `topics.md`.

### 5.2 The Sovereignty Seal as candidate demand-side instrument (2 min, 1 slide)

- Corpus chain: Seal → BAS → Chapter VII contains VII.5 "Strategic access to innovation procurement."
- The extension: the Seal is ambiguous by design — it doesn't say "for procurement", it says "strategically important." That ambiguity lets it work across investor + procurement channels. A Commission-issued credential that Member State procurement authorities could pick up under existing procurement law, *without* triggering Article 107 state-aid review.
- **Register:** this is the question the work leaves me with, not a claim it makes. VII.5 body was outside the sliced primary corpus. Position as reflection, not finding.
- Adjacencies (name, don't overclaim): Mazzucato, Karo & Kattel, Draghi, Letta.

### 5.3 Practice implications (1 min, 1 slide)

- Consolidated LO #6 answer.
- For a **policy officer** designing the next instrument: the WP 2026 architecture shows demand-side statecraft as the under-designed side; the Seal is a candidate design pattern for future instruments.
- For an **EIC evaluator**: the Seal is not a consolation prize; it's an operational credential. Treat it as separable from the investment decision (which is how the WP already positions it).
- For a **would-be STEP applicant**: the Seal has downstream value (InvestEU + BAS gateway; procurement discretion signal) independent of whether STEP funds the round.
