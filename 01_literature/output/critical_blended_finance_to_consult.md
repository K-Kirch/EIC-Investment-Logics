# Critical voices on EU blended finance — to consult

**Purpose.** The thesis takes a critical institutional stance on Pathfinder
and STEP. The §1.2 framing rests on the claim that the wider literature on
EU blended finance sits "almost uniformly in a policy-advocacy register".
That claim is defensible only if Ch 2 acknowledges the existing critical
strand and locates the thesis within it. This file lists the critical
voices that need to be read in original and engaged with before the
framing can be locked.

**Status.** Reading list. None of these are yet in `bibliography.bib`.
Promote to a bibliography entry only after the source has been read.

## Priority 1 — read before Ch 2 is finalised

### Daniela Gabor — the "Wall Street Consensus" / de-risking state

**Why it matters.** Gabor is the single most consequential critical voice
on the architecture the thesis is reading. The Wall Street Consensus
argument is that catalytic public capital underwrites private returns at
public risk, financialising what is presented as a developmental
intervention. Generalises directly from development-finance contexts to
EU blended finance and to STEP-style equity catalysis.

**Key works to consult.**
- Gabor, D. (2021). "The Wall Street Consensus." *Development and Change*
  52(3): 429--459.
- Gabor, D. and Sylla, N. S. (2023). "Derisking developmentalism."
  Subsequent / related pieces extending the argument to industrial
  policy.

**Where it bites the thesis.**
- STEP's anchor-investor design (catalytic equity, crowding-in language)
  is exactly the architecture Gabor critiques.
- Ch 5's circular-market-validation argument is congruent with Gabor's
  "state constructs the asset class it then de-risks" reading and should
  cite her in support.
- Ch 1 §1.2's "policy-advocacy register" claim survives a viva only if
  Gabor is engaged as the named exception in Ch 2.

### Mertens & Thiemann — the EIB and the hidden developmental state

**Why it matters.** Treats EU financial institutions as objects of
institutional analysis rather than as policy advocates. Reads what the
institution does against what it says about itself. The methodological
posture is close to the thesis's own.

**Key works to consult.**
- Mertens, D. and Thiemann, M. (2018 / 2019). Work on the European
  Investment Bank as a hidden developmental state. Verify exact
  citations from journal listings before citing.
- Related: Mertens, D., Thiemann, M., and Volberding, P. (eds.) (2021).
  *The Reinvention of Development Banking in the European Union.*
  Oxford University Press.

**Where it bites the thesis.**
- Methodologically: gives a published precedent for reading EU
  financial vehicles against their own self-description.
- Substantively: situates the EIC Fund and STEP Scale Up inside the
  wider European hidden-developmental-state architecture, which
  strengthens Ch 5's developmental-state framing.
- Ch 2 §sec:bg-mazzucato can be tightened by setting Mazzucato's
  entrepreneurial-state argument against Mertens & Thiemann's
  empirical reading of how EU vehicles actually operate.

## Priority 2 — useful, not blocking

### Angela Wigger — industrial policy as re-entrenchment

**Why it matters.** Reads the EU's reformist turn as continuation of
neoliberal corporate-interest entrenchment under a developmental veneer.
Sharper political-economy register than Gabor; less directly about the
financial mechanics.

**Key works to consult.**
- Wigger, A. (2023). "The New EU Industrial Policy and Deepening
  Structural Asymmetries: Smart Specialisation Not So Smart."
  *Journal of Common Market Studies*. Verify volume / issue.

### Karo & Kattel — internal critique of the entrepreneurial state

**Why it matters.** Inside the Mazzucato tradition, but argues that
absorptive-capacity asymmetries across Member States mean the
entrepreneurial-state model does not travel uniformly. Useful for
Ch 5's boundary-conditions section.

**Key works to consult.**
- Karo, E. and Kattel, R. Various pieces on innovation policy capacity
  and absorptive capacity in CEE / post-socialist states.

   inscription, and the thesis is constructive with her as a named
   ally."
